Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Badam Satti (Seven of Hearts)

Developer: Orange Ocean Labs  |  Effective Date: November 15, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Welcome to Badam Satti (also known as Seven of Hearts or Badam Satti 7), developed and published by Orange Ocean Labs ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you play our mobile card game application (the "App").

Privacy Summary: Badam Satti does not require you to create an account, sign up with an email address or phone number, or share any sensitive personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to any third parties.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

We collect and process only the minimum functional data necessary to let you play single-player AI matches and real-time online multiplayer matches:

  • Player Display Name (Nickname): When you enter a display name on the main menu, it is used solely to identify your seat on the card table during gameplay and on the round scoreboard. Your chosen nickname is stored locally on your device so you do not have to re-type it each time you launch the App.
  • Random Local Player Identifier & App Preferences: The App generates a random, non-personal player identifier (for example, p_xxxxxxx) and saves your audio mute/unmute preference in local device storage (Android SharedPreferences). This identifier is used strictly to maintain your seat connection in active online rooms.
  • Ephemeral Multiplayer Game State: When you play in Private Game or Public Game mode, temporary game state data—consisting of the room code, player display names, active cards on the board, turn index, and round scores—is synchronized in real time via Google Firebase so all players at the table see the same cards and turns.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or government ID.
  • We do not access or collect your precise or coarse GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, media files, or call logs.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell any user information.

3. Device Permissions Used by the App

Badam Satti requests only standard, non-invasive Android permissions required for core gameplay:

  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used strictly to connect to our Firebase real-time game relay for Private and Public multiplayer rooms and to notify you if your internet connection drops.
  • Screen Wake Lock (Keep Screen On): While an active card match is in progress, the App prevents the screen from dimming or locking mid-turn. As soon as you return to the main menu or exit the match, the screen wake lock is released.

4. Data Retention and Automatic Deletion

  • Multiplayer Rooms Are Temporary: Online game rooms (Private and Public) are ephemeral. Once a match ends or all human players leave the room, the room data and match state are automatically deleted from the Firebase server within seconds.
  • Local Device Data: Your saved nickname and mute setting remain on your phone until you change them, clear the App's storage in your Android system settings, or uninstall the App.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase (Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Firestore) solely as a real-time infrastructure service to synchronize card plays between connected players in online multiplayer rooms. Google's processing of infrastructure data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. No Real-Money Gambling or Financial Transactions

Badam Satti is a free-to-play traditional card game designed purely for casual entertainment with friends, family, and AI bots. The App contains no real-money gambling, betting, wagering, cash prizes, or financial transactions of any kind. All scores in the game are virtual card penalty points with zero monetary value.

7. Children's Privacy

Badam Satti is a general-audience traditional card game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). Furthermore, an automated profanity filter is applied to player display names in multiplayer modes to help maintain a family-friendly environment. If you believe a child has entered personal information into a display name, please contact us and we will promptly assist you.

8. Data Security

All communication between the App and our Firebase multiplayer servers is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Because we do not collect personal accounts or sensitive user records, your exposure to personal data risk is minimized by design.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the App or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
